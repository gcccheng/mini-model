继续训练报告 / Continued training report
===========================================

原因 / Reason
-------------
500-step 模型只观察了约 36% 的一遍训练语料，生成结果缺乏稳定诗句结构。
The 500-step model had seen only about 36% of one training-corpus pass and did
not produce stable poetic structure.

训练设置 / Training setup
-------------------------
起点 / Starting checkpoint: step 500
终点 / Final step: 3,000
模型参数 / Model parameters: 10,032,128
设备 / Device: Apple M5 MPS
累计训练 token / Cumulative training tokens: 12,288,000
累计语料 epoch / Approximate cumulative corpus epochs: 2.16
累计耗时 / Cumulative elapsed time: 609.37 seconds
续训学习率 / Continuation learning rate: cosine decay from about 1.13e-4 to 2e-5

同参数生成样本 / Same-settings generation sample
------------------------------------------------
朝代：唐朝
作者：李白
题目：月夜
正文：天涯一夜，夜晚秋霜。白云中夜，霜夜月秋。秋凉又，梦落花寒。

结论 / Conclusion
-----------------
新模型已明显学到诗句边界、常见节奏及更稳定的短句模式，但 10M 参数模型、
混合体裁语料和约 2.16 epoch 仍不足以持续生成高质量格律诗。输出应视为模型
生成的诗歌草稿，不应冒充李白原作。

The continued model has learned clearer line boundaries, common rhythms, and
more stable short phrases. However, a 10M-parameter model trained on mixed genres
for about 2.16 epochs still cannot consistently write high-quality regulated verse.
Outputs are generated drafts and must not be presented as authentic Li Bai works.
