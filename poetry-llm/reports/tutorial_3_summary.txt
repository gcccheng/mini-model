完整性保证 / Integrity guarantees
----------------------------------
- 所有源记录均有去向；被排除的数据保存在审计文件中。
  Every source record is accounted for; excluded data remains in audit files.
- 训练、验证和测试 ID 无重叠。
  Train, validation, and test IDs do not overlap.
- 相同正文（即使标题不同）不会跨集合出现。
  Identical bodies, even under different titles, cannot cross split boundaries.
- 最终记录没有 ASCII 字母/数字编码残片及已知缺字占位符。
  Final records contain no ASCII alphanumeric encoding fragments or known
  missing-character placeholders.
- 所有脚本使用固定规则，可重复生成相同结果。
  All outputs are deterministically reproducible from fixed rules.
