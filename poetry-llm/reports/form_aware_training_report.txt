体裁感知训练报告 / Form-aware training report
================================================

问题 / Problem
--------------
通用模型没有体裁标签，因此不能可靠地区分五言、七言、绝句和律诗。
The general model had no form labels and could not reliably distinguish five- or
seven-character lines, quatrains, and regulated verse.

自动检查样本 / Automatically checked samples: 16
行数和字数检查 / Line-count and character-count check: PASS

实现方式 / Implementation
-------------------------
1. 模型输入增加“体裁：五言绝句”等显式条件。
   The model input receives an explicit form condition such as “体裁：五言绝句”.
2. 只使用行数、字数和标点全部严格匹配的记录进行专项训练。
   Fine-tuning uses only records whose line count, width, and punctuation match strictly.
3. generate_form.py 在采样时只允许汉字，并强制准确的行数、行宽和“，。”结构。
   generate_form.py samples only Han characters and enforces exact line count,
   line width, and comma/full-stop structure.

限制 / Limitations
------------------
当前约束保证“形式正确”，但不保证平仄、押韵、对仗、典故或语义质量。
The current constraints guarantee structural form, but not tonal patterns, rhyme,
parallelism, allusions, or semantic quality.
